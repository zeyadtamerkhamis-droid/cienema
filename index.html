About CineAI Egypt

A premium booking experience, built for Egypt

CineAI Egypt brings automated seat suggestions, instant validation, and a modern digital ticket to Egyptian cinema-goers. This standalone build runs entirely in your browser — booked seats are remembered on this device, and every confirmed booking sends a notification email to the cinema team.
